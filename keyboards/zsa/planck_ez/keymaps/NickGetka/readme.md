# NickGetka Planck Layout

## Quick reference

Hold LOWER or RAISE for the following layouts, or both for ADJUST. `___` falls
through to the next active layer. Symbol output assumes a US host layout.

```text
LOWER
 ~    `    !    @    #    $    %    ^    &    *    _   Bksp
Ctrl  |    \    +    ;    =    <    >    (    )    {    }
Shift -    /    ?    :    '    "    ,    .    [    ]   Enter

RAISE
 %    -    1    2    3    =   Home PgDn PgUp  End  Del  Bksp
Ctrl  0    4    5    6    *   Left Down  Up  Right ___  ___
Shift +    7    8    9    .    ___  ___  ___  ___  ___ Enter
 /   Ctrl+Alt Alt  GUI  LOWER   Space    RAISE Left Up Down Right

ADJUST
 ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  PrtSc RGB
 ___  Mute Vol- Vol+ C+A+L ___ Prev C+A+R Play Next ___ ___
 ___  Bri- Bri+ ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___  ___ ___
 Boot ___  ___  ___ LOWER   Space    RAISE ___  ___  ___ ___
```

`C+A+L` and `C+A+R` send Ctrl+Alt+Left and Ctrl+Alt+Right. Media, brightness
and screenshot keys depend on host support.

On the Planck EZ, the left and right front indicators light while LOWER and
RAISE are held, respectively. Both light when ADJUST is active.
Audio is enabled at startup and the Planck startup song plays after a short
delay.

On the Planck rev6 Drop, the LOWER and RAISE LEDs light white while their
respective layers are active, even when underglow is off. Brightness is capped
at 25/255 (about 10%). Underglow starts off; hold LOWER and RAISE and press the
top-right key to toggle it for the session.
